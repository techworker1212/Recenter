Recenter

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

In short: Recenter does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your time limits, your messages and your app usage stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers.

Who we are

Recenter is an app made by Techworker ("we", "us"). It lets you set a time limit and a personal reminder for the apps you choose. When the limit is reached, Recenter shows your reminder and then pauses that app until you choose to unlock it.

Information we collect

We don't collect any information. Recenter has no user accounts and never sends data to us or to anyone else. It doesn't include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Information that stays on your device

To work, Recenter keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your rules: the apps you picked, the label you gave each one, its time limit, and the notification title and message you wrote.
  • Your goals for today, and which ones you've ticked off. They're cleared each day.
  • Your preferences: each app's unlock time, and light or dark mode.
  • A daily count of reminders ("interruptions today"), shown on the home screen.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't uploaded anywhere.

Screen Time on iPhone

On iPhone, Recenter uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings), with your permission, to measure time in the apps you choose and to pause them. Apple gives Recenter only anonymous tokens for the apps you pick. Recenter can't see app names, your browsing, or what you do inside other apps. Usage measurement happens on your device, inside Apple's system. You can turn off Recenter's access at any time in Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access.

Usage access on Android

On Android, Recenter asks for Usage Access so it can tell which app is open and apply that app's limit. It also asks to display over other apps so it can show its pause screen. This information is read and used only on your device, and is never stored beyond what's needed for your limits or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Recenter sends local notifications that contain the reminder text you wrote. They're created on your device. Recenter doesn't use push notification servers.

Sharing and selling

We don't share or sell any data, because we don't have any.

Children

Recenter doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Deleting your data

Deleting a rule in the app removes it immediately. Deleting the Recenter app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If Recenter ever starts collecting data, we'll explain what and why here before that happens.
