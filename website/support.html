Common questions

Why does Recenter need Screen Time access?

Apple only lets apps measure time in other apps and pause them through Screen Time. Recenter asks for it once, and you confirm with your iPhone passcode. It only sees the apps you choose, as anonymous tokens. It never sees app names or what you do inside them. See the privacy policy.

Why do I have to type a name for the app I picked?

To protect your privacy, Apple doesn't tell Recenter which app you picked. The name you type, such as "Instagram", is only used inside Recenter so you can recognise your rules.

How is my time counted?

On iPhone, Recenter counts your total time in that app during the day, not one sitting. Closing and reopening the app doesn't reset it. The count starts when you turn on monitoring or save the rule, and then resets each day.

My reminder didn't appear, or it was late
  • Check that Monitoring is on in Recenter and that the rule's switch is on.
  • Check that notifications are allowed: Settings → Notifications → Recenter.
  • Check that Screen Time access is on: Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access.
  • iOS decides exactly when to report usage, so the reminder can sometimes arrive a minute or so after your limit. This is normal.
How do I unlock an app that Recenter paused?

On the pause screen, tap Unlock. The app opens again for that app's unlock time, and then Recenter checks in again. Each app has its own unlock time (2 minutes unless you change it): tap the app's rule and use the Unlock for slider.

How do today's goals work?

Add a few goals for the day on Recenter's home screen, like "Read 20 pages". Your next unfinished goal appears with every reminder and on the pause screen. To tick one off, tap it in Recenter, or press and hold the reminder notification and tap Done. Each day starts with a fresh list.

Does everything reset each day?

Yes. At midnight, paused apps are unpaused, every app gets its full limit again, and the interruption count and goals start fresh.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Tap the moon or sun button at the top of Recenter's home screen.

How do I change or remove a limit?
  • To edit the limit or reminder, tap the rule on Recenter's home screen.
  • To turn a rule off, use the switch next to it.
  • To delete a rule, tap the bin icon.
  • To turn everything off, switch off Monitoring.
How do I stop using Recenter completely?

Turn off Monitoring, then delete the app. Deleting Recenter removes its limits and all its data from your iPhone. You can also turn off its access in Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access.
